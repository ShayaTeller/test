# Product

POSSIBLE is a Hebrew-language marketing/landing page for a custom software development agency. The site targets Israeli businesses and entrepreneurs who have a product idea and need a tech partner to build it.

## Core Value Proposition
Turn ideas into real products — covering web apps, e-commerce sites, CRM systems, custom systems, mobile apps, and integrations/automations.

## Audience
Israeli businesses, entrepreneurs, and startups looking for a technology partner. The entire UI is in Hebrew and laid out RTL (`dir="rtl"`).

## Key Sections
- Hero — main call-to-action
- Services — types of products the agency builds
- Process — 7-step journey from idea to launch
- Why Us — trust signals
- Audience — self-identification checklist
- About — agency background
- Contact form — lead capture (full name, phone, email, project type, message)
